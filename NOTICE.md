# Notice

This repository is an **unofficial, non-commercial reconstruction** of the
front end of `tryplayground.com`, built as a study in measurement-driven
cloning. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Playground.

## What is and isn't mine to license

The **code** here — components, build setup, design tokens, the IA tooling and
the measurement harness described in `PROCESS.md` — is the original work of
this repository.

The **content is not**. Headings, body copy, FAQ questions and answers, product
names and navigation labels were read from the live site and reproduced so that
measured layout and line counts would hold. Those remain Playground's.

## What is deliberately absent

No brand assets are committed. `.gitignore` excludes:

- `public/assets/` — 671 downloaded images, including five named customer
  organisations' logos, photographs of identifiable children and teachers, and
  hand-drawn portraits of named individuals. Third-party marks and likenesses
  are not the site owner's to relicense, and are not redistributed here.
- `reference/` — full-page screenshots of the live original.

The app therefore renders with missing images on a fresh clone. That is
intended. `PROCESS.md` documents how the assets were obtained if you have your
own authorization to fetch them.
